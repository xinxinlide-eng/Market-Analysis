# Market Stall

A small web app for selling products at markets: keep track of stock, record sales on each selling day, check the cash and PayPal totals at the end of the day, and see which products to buy more of.

## How to use

Open `index.html` in a browser (works on a phone too). You don't need to install anything.

- **Products**: add products with a photo or screenshot, a category, the price in RMB, an estimated shipping fee per item (€) and how many are in stock. The app converts the price to euros and shows the cost per item.
- **Selling day**: start a day with the date, your small change (notes), coins and PayPal balance. Record each sale: pick the category, the product, the selling price and whether it was paid in cash or with PayPal. At the end of the day you see the total sales and the cash and PayPal amounts you should have. Type in what you actually have to check that it matches.
- **Analysis**: revenue, profit and profit rate; top products; results by category and by day; and tips on what to buy next.
- **Settings**: the RMB → € exchange rate, your categories, and backup export/import.

## Your data

Everything is saved in the browser on the device you use. It is not uploaded anywhere. Use **Settings → Export backup** regularly, and **Import backup** to move your data to another device.
